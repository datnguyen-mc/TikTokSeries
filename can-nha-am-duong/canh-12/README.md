- **Thời gian:** 23:00 Đêm thứ sáu đến Sáng hôm sau (11:00–12:00)
- **Bối cảnh:** Main room wooden bed at night, misty village rice-field cemetery at dawn, newly installed mahogany altar in the main room, and brick shrine under the jackfruit tree.
- **Trục không gian cố định:** Progression from the moonlit main hall bed to Cụ Xá's mossy stone grave in the field, then back to the central mahogany altar and gate jackfruit shrine.
