## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make arrival feel like a deliberate loss of escape, not a travel postcard. **Diễn xuất:** His eyes follow the coach longer than his body does, then settle toward town.
- **2:** Earn Bảy's later kindness through an ordinary helpful act, without making Sơn heroic. **Diễn xuất:** He treats the recovery as routine; Bảy's surprise softens into curiosity rather than gratitude theatre.
- **3:** Show Sơn choosing isolation while Bảy offers practical shelter without demanding a confession. **Diễn xuất:** Sơn asks quietly without selling a cover story; Bảy answers from habit, not suspicion.
- **4:** Make receiving the key feel like accepting a temporary place, not finding safety. **Diễn xuất:** Bảy is lightly winded and matter-of-fact; Sơn receives without the ease of a vacationer.
- **5:** Turn a vague stay into an exact private deadline that the audience cannot yet explain. **Diễn xuất:** Express the decision through a steady wrist and controlled pressure, not a violent flourish.
- **6:** Attach the closed watch to a withheld emotional truth rather than explaining its contents. **Diễn xuất:** His face should resist tears; one contained breath carries more weight than a mournful stare.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Let the porch sea wind fall under rain on the silk-shop awning. Cut to the existing silk curtain, not to another coach arrival.
