## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make Sơn's seating choice a small behavioral mystery that Bảy notices before Mây does. **Diễn xuất:** His road attention is habitual rather than frantic; Bảy's comment carries amused curiosity.
- **2:** Introduce Mây through a precise observation rather than an instant supernatural diagnosis. **Diễn xuất:** Her response is interested and economical; Thành reacts with routine familiarity rather than comic surprise.
- **3:** Let a routine question reveal Sơn's need to control space without making him rude. **Diễn xuất:** Mây is courteous but attentive; Sơn's tidiness should read as reflex, not a staged concealment.
- **4:** Let the offered first name be a concession while keeping the man behind it unreadable. **Diễn xuất:** He states a fact without friendliness or aggression; she accepts it without pretending belief is established.
- **5:** Show Mây converting curiosity into a discreet check while keeping the encounter socially ordinary. **Diễn xuất:** Her voice lowers only enough for privacy; Thành's attentive note is work, not a nervous gag.
- **6:** Leave a trace of controlled unease after Mây departs without adding a new clue. **Diễn xuất:** The movement is precise but not fussy; tension lives in his delayed release of the handle.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Introduce the flower-shop bell over the final coffee-cup detail, then cut into the first visit. No repeated cup movement.
