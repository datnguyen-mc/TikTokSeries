## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Show the professional reason for entering before Hạ makes the encounter personal. **Diễn xuất:** Hạ's greeting is easy and direct; Sơn answers her presence first with a glance past her.
- **2:** Let the first connection grow from competent care rather than instant flirtation. **Diễn xuất:** His reply can be faintly self-mocking; Hạ registers the difference between the guarded man and helpful hands.
- **3:** Make the second visit feel like Hạ recognizing a pattern while giving Sơn room to deny it. **Diễn xuất:** Her smile is suppressed, not coyly performed; his pause should reveal surprise at being remembered.
- **4:** Allow Hạ to notice the surveillance without turning the scene into an accusation. **Diễn xuất:** Curiosity stays warmer than suspicion; Sơn's attention leaves the lobby reluctantly but completely for this beat.
- **5:** Make the third return an offered kindness that Sơn cannot explain as surveillance work. **Diễn xuất:** Sơn avoids pride; Hạ's joke should be affectionate surprise at practical attention, not a laugh track setup.
- **6:** Finish the meeting on Sơn choosing to stay, a small change that will later cost him. **Diễn xuất:** His face softens without an instant grin; her laugh subsides naturally when she realizes he listened.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Carry the shop-door bell resonance briefly into the quiet cabin, cut to the already-open bag and new evidence action.
