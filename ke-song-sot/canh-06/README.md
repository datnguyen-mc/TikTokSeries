## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make Hạ's discovery interrupt an ordinary task before she has language for what she has seen. **Diễn xuất:** Her question begins with contained alarm, not immediate acceptance; Sơn notices the stopped hand before answering.
- **2:** Let Hạ establish a boundary that makes the later gift a choice rather than blind forgiveness. **Diễn xuất:** Her hurt is clear but not pleading; Sơn's inability to answer should feel more exposed than a confession.
- **3:** Make the first bare hand an attempt at honesty whose inadequacy Sơn already understands. **Diễn xuất:** His line should carry uncertainty rather than a promise of instant reform; Hạ listens without rewarding him immediately.
- **4:** Give Hạ's family watch an ordinary working life before it acquires emotional meaning. **Diễn xuất:** Her fingers are familiar and unceremonious, suggesting a family object rather than a mysterious talisman.
- **5:** Make accepting the watch require Sơn's voluntary response, preserving Hạ's agency in the offer. **Diễn xuất:** Her invitation is hopeful but conditional; he receives it with hesitation, not a romantic grin on cue.
- **6:** Finish on an imperfect intention rather than treating the gift as instant transformation. **Diễn xuất:** His promise should contain shame at still needing the glove; Hạ turns off the kettle without a reproachful display.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Let the kettle hiss finish. Cut to the next morning doorframe and its second tally, daylight replacing the warm room.
