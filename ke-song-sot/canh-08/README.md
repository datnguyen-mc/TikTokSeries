## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Create anticipation by letting the expected lift and white car occupy space before the wrong man appears. **Diễn xuất:** His professional patience carries unease only when the lift opens, not from a constant frightened expression.
- **2:** Make Dũng's familiar presence feel wrong through his handling of the envelope and blocked exit. **Diễn xuất:** His casual instruction should sound rehearsed; Sơn's body stays contained rather than immediately attacking.
- **3:** Let recognition of the planted evidence change the meaning of the meeting before the chase begins. **Diễn xuất:** Recognition should appear through arrested fingers and a controlled retreat, not an invented face reaction within the insert.
- **4:** Turn the trap into immediate physical pressure and damage the watch without making it an action trophy. **Diễn xuất:** Fear shows as a sudden narrowed breath, then he regains control instead of posing through danger.
- **5:** Make escape depend on taking a reachable maintenance exit, preserving the clothes and object continuity. **Diễn xuất:** He is economical but physically taxed; a delayed inhale after the closure keeps him human.
- **6:** Let the silent watch create a private loss before Sơn discovers a greater one at the flower shop. **Diễn xuất:** His voice-over stays retrospective and dry; present expression is concentration giving way to alarm, not performed grief yet.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** City rain falls away, the arriving black car establishes present-day afternoon. Do not replay the garage escape.
