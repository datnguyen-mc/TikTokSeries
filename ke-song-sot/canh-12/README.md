## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Turn the familiar morning tally into an alarmed departure without replaying earlier carving beats. **Diễn xuất:** The tool hand becomes economical, not panicked; the choice of exit should show practiced awareness.
- **2:** Let the intruders arrive a beat too late, making the empty cabin itself the suspenseful subject. **Diễn xuất:** They should be wary and impatient rather than inept; no named lead secretly lingers in the room.
- **3:** Show Sơn choosing control over injury, while retaining the physical difficulty of stopping a pursuer. **Diễn xuất:** His breath tightens with effort; the pursuer resists instead of instantly becoming a limp stunt prop.
- **4:** Escalate from a controlled restraint to an unpredictable second threat, not a display of effortless mastery. **Diễn xuất:** Show surprise in his breath and shoulders; the second man overcommits rather than politely waiting for a move.
- **5:** Make the decisive moral action a call for help after the opponent has become vulnerable. **Diễn xuất:** His urgency is practical concern rather than guilt performed at camera; he does not admire the result.
- **6:** End the action on Sơn accepting scrutiny, making cooperation a cost he consciously pays. **Diễn xuất:** Mây's command is firm but not triumphant; Sơn obeys without a defiant stare or another attempted escape.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Carry the fallen man breathing under the cut to the clinic. No replay of the fall or stretcher arrival.
