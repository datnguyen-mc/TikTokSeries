## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make care precede interrogation so the clinic scene changes the meaning of the preceding violence. **Diễn xuất:** His line is calm practical attention; Mây absorbs the need for patience rather than demanding instant answers.
- **2:** Make recognition of Tê depend on a remembered physical match, not instant laboratory omniscience. **Diễn xuất:** His fingers pause at the cap shape with cautious recognition, not certainty about every medical consequence.
- **3:** Protect future suspense by refusing an exact recovery clock while keeping the doctor useful. **Diễn xuất:** His firmness should be weary experience rather than a lecture, and her revision an acceptance of limits.
- **4:** Let a suspect's silence become evidence of recognition without pretending it is a full confession. **Diễn xuất:** Mây asks plainly; the attacker stops answering through a tightened mouth, not a villainous laugh.
- **5:** Let an off-screen slip of the nickname redirect Mây toward the person with the ledger. **Diễn xuất:** Her face narrows into a decision, not a sudden gasp; she does not mouth the off-screen man's sentence.
- **6:** Show that seeking Sơn does not mean abandoning the people and evidence already in custody. **Diễn xuất:** Her authority is clear and workmanlike; Thành's acknowledgement should be competent rather than needlessly anxious.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Station radio fades into porch wind. Cut to Mây joining the waiting Sơn and placing her cuffs once.
