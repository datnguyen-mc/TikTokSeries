## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make the negotiation's moral boundary visible before either person asks for trust. **Diễn xuất:** Her question is direct without provocation; Sơn acknowledges the name through attention, not a smug pose.
- **2:** Let Sơn offer something independently checkable, shifting from evasion toward responsibility. **Diễn xuất:** Express the risk in the deliberate withdrawal; his off-screen sentence is an invitation to verify, not a demand for belief.
- **3:** Give Mây a small verified success before the personal shock arrives, preserving her professional basis for cooperation. **Diễn xuất:** Her line conveys procedure, not a promise of protection; Sơn waits without trying to charm or hurry her.
- **4:** Let Sơn accept cooperation but expose the unresolved human stake behind the financial evidence. **Diễn xuất:** He should sound more exposed when mentioning the held person than when admitting the ledger is his.
- **5:** Make Mây's recognition land through touch and hesitation while keeping the watch's future reveal intact. **Diễn xuất:** Her fingers momentarily firm at the edge; the off-screen recognition is quieter than a shouted shock.
- **6:** Turn professional cooperation into personal accountability without letting either person escape the moment. **Diễn xuất:** The question carries restrained hurt rather than immediate tears; his refusal to look away is the answer before words.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** A ferry horn enters while Mây waits for an answer. Cut to Hạ alive at the city pier, not to a second photo reveal.
