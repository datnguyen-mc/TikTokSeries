## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make returning to Hạ's real place shift the investigation from stories to physical geography. **Diễn xuất:** She moves with local familiarity rather than performing a tour; his pace is attentive, not nostalgic ownership.
- **2:** Let Mây place Hạ's absence inside a real home and a real friendship, not just Sơn's grief. **Diễn xuất:** Her tone is matter-of-fact with held concern; his reaction should not claim he knows Hạ's home better than she does.
- **3:** Let Sơn state an assumption as his own fear rather than establishing Hạ's death as objective truth. **Diễn xuất:** His voice should be reluctant and ashamed, not a definitive narrator's verdict; she listens without accepting the premise.
- **4:** Make Mây's refusal of an unsupported conclusion feel like an act of care grounded in evidence. **Diễn xuất:** Her line is firm and steady, carrying hope through a practical verb; his release of the phone accepts her method.
- **5:** Plant a working public fixture quietly, so its later use feels earned rather than magically discovered. **Diễn xuất:** Her hand is familiar and routine, not secretive; no face reaction should announce a future rescue device.
- **6:** Close the visit with a concrete next investigative step, keeping the geography and key custody clear. **Diễn xuất:** Her urgency is procedural rather than panic; he follows her direction without another speech about loss.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** The clipped service key settles, incoming station fan introduces the USB desk. Do not replay the backup-lamp test.
