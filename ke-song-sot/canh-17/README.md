## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make the investigation's first digital step preserve trust in the evidence instead of treating a USB as magic. **Diễn xuất:** Thành's confidence comes from knowing his task; Sơn waits rather than taking over someone else's expertise.
- **2:** Let one matching sequence connect money to the factory, avoiding a stream of unfilmable numbers. **Diễn xuất:** His hands briefly slow at the match; the off-screen finding is restrained because more remains locked.
- **3:** Separate Kiên's money from Bà Hai's logistics so the later conflict has identifiable interests. **Diễn xuất:** He speaks as someone admitting insider knowledge, not delivering a detective's victorious explanation.
- **4:** Make the locked dispatch detail a remaining obstacle, preserving the later proof of Hạ's transfer. **Diễn xuất:** Her hand pauses at the locked portion, showing the limit of what they know rather than certainty about Hạ's name.
- **5:** Turn the obstruction into a specific risky next step that depends on Sơn's past access. **Diễn xuất:** Sơn's recognition should carry reluctance at returning to Bà Hai, while Thành focuses on retaining a practical description.
- **6:** End the investigation scene on an accountable choice to leave town with protection arranged, not on more exposition. **Diễn xuất:** Her concern for Hạ stays under professional control; Thành accepts responsibility without another comic objection.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Close the night station sound cleanly. New morning engines establish Dũng arriving with changed stakes, not a repeated visitor search.
