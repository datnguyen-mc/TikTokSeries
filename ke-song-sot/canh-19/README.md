## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Let matching torn edges create stronger evidence while preserving what the cloth cannot tell them. **Diễn xuất:** His fingers hesitate at near contact, carrying longing that the factual line alone should not overstate.
- **2:** Let the fourth tally register a new day of pressure without turning counting into a repeated ritual montage. **Diễn xuất:** His movement is more decisive than the first day's, but not angry enough to damage the whole frame.
- **3:** Make evidence custody part of growing trust, while leaving Sơn a small private piece to carry. **Diễn xuất:** Neither should handle the cloth sentimentally for too long; the care comes through precision and mutual permission.
- **4:** Show Mây joining the risk by preparation, not by abandoning her responsibility or instantly changing clothes. **Diễn xuất:** Her resolve is practical; Sơn's attention shifts from the cloth to the preparation without another warning gesture.
- **5:** Let Mây define her own reason for going, preventing the trip from becoming Sơn's possessive rescue narrative. **Diễn xuất:** Her correction is steady rather than combative; his response should be acceptance of her agency, not offended pride.
- **6:** Let an unanswered phone interrupt a completed choice, not manufacture the reason they suddenly travel. **Diễn xuất:** His finger should hover briefly from caution, not because he is performing a gadget reveal.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** The phone vibration stops when answered. Cut wider for speaker mode, without a second ring or second answering gesture.
