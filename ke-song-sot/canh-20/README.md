## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make the caller's access to a personal detail unsettle Sơn while Mây preserves room for doubt. **Diễn xuất:** Sơn's reaction is contained; Mây watches him as much as the phones, not accepting the claim as proof.
- **2:** Expose the hope Sơn cannot suppress without letting it become certainty that Hạ is alive. **Diễn xuất:** His eyes sharpen before the voice changes; the question should reveal need that he would prefer to hide.
- **3:** Let the caller convert that hope into an assignment, making manipulation legible in the same held reaction. **Diễn xuất:** His expression should close slightly as he recognizes the steering; do not add a new shocked response.
- **4:** Give Mây a visible intervention before Sơn can be drawn into a new bargain. **Diễn xuất:** Her tone is low and firm rather than an order shouted over him; he hears the practical boundary.
- **5:** Let Sơn reclaim the terms by demanding evidence and ending contact himself. **Diễn xuất:** The firmness should cost him something; he still wants the caller to be right, but will not perform another job blindly.
- **6:** Close the act on an already-chosen journey, preserving the cost of leaving Thành responsible for home. **Diễn xuất:** Their departures are purposeful rather than romantic; Thành watches as someone receiving responsibility, not waving at a holiday trip.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Keep the same departing coach engine into the on-board view. No second boarding or repeat door close.
