## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make receiving Hạ's image interrupt an escape that had briefly found a rhythm. **Diễn xuất:** His urgency shows through careful handling, while Mây waits rather than grabbing the phone.
- **2:** Let Hạ use the forced recording as an opportunity to send her own information. **Diễn xuất:** She speaks evenly for whoever holds the camera, then lets her eyes briefly indicate the clue.
- **3:** Turn proof of survival into evidence that Hạ is helping the search. **Diễn xuất:** Mây's recognition is quiet and specific, while Sơn has to move from relief back to attention.
- **4:** Make the caller try to reclaim control immediately after Hạ has reclaimed a little agency. **Diễn xuất:** His restraint hardens; the decision to end the line is small but unequivocal.
- **5:** Convert Hạ's clue into a useful route while preserving uncertainty about the actual room. **Diễn xuất:** Her confidence is limited to what matches, resisting a triumphant declaration of exact location.
- **6:** Show a commitment to a shared rescue plan rather than another solitary revenge vow. **Diễn xuất:** The line is a plan spoken to a partner, not narration addressed to the audience.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** River wind bridges into corridor rain in the distant memory. Do not bring back phone-video fan noise after that video has ended.
