## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Keep pursuit suspense in distance and obstruction rather than multiplying attacks. **Diễn xuất:** Urgency stays in pace and breath; he does not check behind with repeated dramatic head turns.
- **2:** Make the townspeople useful by closing a route after a person escapes, then protecting themselves. **Diễn xuất:** Their concentration is practical, with no cheering or desire to fight the pursuers.
- **3:** Show support arriving where evacuation needs it rather than materializing to win a fight. **Diễn xuất:** Thành stays useful despite damaged glasses; Mây checks the exit before watching an arrest.
- **4:** Let Dũng recover pressure through time after losing immediate control of the aisle. **Diễn xuất:** He speaks as someone willing to wait for the deadline, making stillness threatening.
- **5:** Move the story from chase to dangerous negotiation through Dũng's withdrawal. **Diễn xuất:** The invitation is delivered as a demand that will outlast this retreat, not a defeated snarl.
- **6:** Make restoring protection an acknowledgement of cost instead of another empty tactical assurance. **Diễn xuất:** She speaks directly and quietly, he accepts responsibility while still recovering from fear.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Let market noise end before establishing night lighthouse wind and Bà Hai already waiting. No second market confrontation.
