## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Turn help from the town into bounded jobs, not a speech summoning a heroic crowd. **Diễn xuất:** She is firm about keeping civilians away, both men listening as workers with specific tasks.
- **2:** Make the eventual rescue vehicle usable through visible ordinary preparation. **Diễn xuất:** Both work with familiar competence, not frantic montage speed or jokes about the coming danger.
- **3:** Let Thành regain agency by checking what he can genuinely see from above. **Diễn xuất:** Concentration has a small physical cost through repaired glasses, but he does not play incompetence.
- **4:** Show the present-day refusal of the gloves as a settled choice rather than a new costume reveal. **Diễn xuất:** He should not linger admiring leather or rehearse the gesture again; resolve has replaced the earlier hesitation.
- **5:** Let walking openly register as exposure earned by the earlier decision to stay. **Diễn xuất:** His shoulders carry tension despite composure; no knowing looks to unseen admirers or heroic smile.
- **6:** Make facing the sea an emotional change that Bảy notices without explaining the whole arc. **Diễn xuất:** His line is observation with quiet care, Sơn receiving it without needing a reply.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** The coffee glass settles, the approaching white-car engine begins before the street cut. Kiên arrives only once.
