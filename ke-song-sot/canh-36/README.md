## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Let Kiên enter the ordinary place Sơn has only just learned to occupy peacefully. **Diễn xuất:** His ease should intrude on Sơn's quiet without a grand villain introduction or theatrical pause.
- **2:** Make Kiên explain control as a business adjustment, while Sơn refuses to reward the explanation. **Diễn xuất:** Kiên speaks easily until naming Hạ; Sơn's smallest attention change reveals the leverage works.
- **3:** Give Hạ an act of self-definition the instant she becomes physically visible again. **Diễn xuất:** She meets Sơn directly, speaking as a capable person rather than asking to be carried away.
- **4:** Make Sơn insist that proof of life must become a visible movement toward freedom. **Diễn xuất:** Sơn states the condition steadily, showing the effort of not grabbing at the first sight of her.
- **5:** Make the demanded confession reveal what Kiên wants bought beyond the ledger. **Diễn xuất:** Kiên treats the coercion as routine paperwork, his practiced smile hiding impatience at resistance.
- **6:** Leave the new demand physically behind after its owner removes himself. **Diễn xuất:** Sơn watches the retreat with held urgency, not relief, because Hạ is still inside.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** White-car engine recedes under the rear-stall discussion. Keep the car gone and the confession folder with Mây.
