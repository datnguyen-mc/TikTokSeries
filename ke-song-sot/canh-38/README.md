## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make the remembered kindness believable enough that rejecting Bà Hai later will hurt. **Diễn xuất:** He is hungry and wary, she practical and attentive rather than visibly plotting a future crime.
- **2:** Show the first permission to receive care before later gifts acquire obligations. **Diễn xuất:** He watches for refusal before the first mouthful, while she lets him have the space.
- **3:** Let useful teaching become a route into belonging before it hardens into control. **Diễn xuất:** He is eager to do it correctly, she approving without the coldness the viewer now expects.
- **4:** Reveal the gloves as a gift whose condition is spoken at the same moment. **Diễn xuất:** His hands accept carefully, unaware of how the gesture will later be understood.
- **5:** Make the cost of shelter emerge as a physical arrangement he cannot ignore. **Diễn xuất:** His expression shifts from wanting approval toward recognizing dependence, while she quietly watches.
- **6:** Make the present hand capable of leaving the gift behind rather than reenacting the first acceptance. **Diễn xuất:** The movement is calm but final, not a ceremonial rejection thrown at the memory.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Present-day bag zipper finishes under the cut to the harbour overview, then sea wind takes over. Gloves stay in the cabin.
