## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Put care and uncertainty on screen without inventing a medical countdown or miracle intervention. **Diễn xuất:** He speaks slowly enough for Sơn to follow; concern appears in sustained attention rather than alarm acting.
- **2:** Show Hạ buying seconds through selective knowledge, with Kiên focused on the wrong problem. **Diễn xuất:** Her practical tone conceals calculation, Kiên resents needing instruction from the person he controls.
- **3:** Pay off clearance of the lower barrier while preserving the remaining distance to Hạ. **Diễn xuất:** Their urgency is disciplined; Thành's concise report sounds like progress, not a premature rescue announcement.
- **4:** Give Hạ a small decisive action the captor does not notice and the audience can remember. **Diễn xuất:** Her hand is precise without displaying triumph, no pause to admire the trick or look into camera.
- **5:** Make Mây offer concrete reassurance based on the team she can actually hear. **Diễn xuất:** Mây's concern is private but her words remain factual; Sơn registers the weight without opening the lid.
- **6:** Make Mây choose the threatened doorway before Dũng can reach the patient again. **Diễn xuất:** Her posture is economical and balanced; Dũng's demand sounds like obedience curdled into ownership.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** The van-door latch leads directly into Dũng first advance. Do not introduce him arriving a second time.
