## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make the climb feel purposeful while preserving Sơn's limited condition and Mây's leadership. **Diễn xuất:** His concentration appears in rigid shoulders and careful breathing; her attention remains on the road.
- **2:** Carry the approach directly into an organized entry rather than another heroic arrival. **Diễn xuất:** Mây keeps an eye on Sơn's balance, he accepts the slower pace without repeating a protest.
- **3:** Let every gained step show that Sơn is participating without pretending to be physically restored. **Diễn xuất:** He is frustrated by the delay but not theatrically collapsing; she reads the pause and stays calm.
- **4:** Reward Hạ's concealed action by making the failed closure visible to her and the audience. **Diễn xuất:** Hạ watches without revealing satisfaction; Kiên interprets the resistance as old machinery, not a plotted trick.
- **5:** Make a tiny survival decision become an actual route for other people. **Diễn xuất:** Their restraint contrasts with Kiên's earlier urgency, and Thành's report carries recognition rather than praise.
- **6:** Place Mây at the threshold of conversation while Sơn visibly remains behind her. **Diễn xuất:** Her tone is steady enough to invite a response; Sơn listens rather than answering Kiên on her behalf.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Keep wind and restrained radio traffic through the parallel cut to Bà Hai arrest below. The climb continues off screen.
