## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Show Bà Hai's authority draining through the ordinary act of having nothing useful to order. **Diễn xuất:** Her composure remains practiced, but the delay before releasing the holder reveals the lost habit of control.
- **2:** Make Dũng ask for an instruction his loyalty can no longer produce. **Diễn xuất:** The line carries fatigue and an unwanted need for her approval, not a new violent challenge.
- **3:** Reduce the symbol of ownership to an object that no longer protects its owner. **Diễn xuất:** Hands are deliberate rather than trembling theatrically; hesitation may appear before the final release.
- **4:** Keep the claim of motherhood present without allowing it to function as a defence against responsibility. **Diễn xuất:** Her sentence sounds like a fact she wants to regain power, not an appeal the scene endorses.
- **5:** Let the law acknowledge her words while refusing to turn them into absolution. **Diễn xuất:** The response lands as an unwanted boundary; she contains the reaction rather than producing a confession speech.
- **6:** Close her physical route without claiming the whole film has ended. **Diễn xuất:** Her pace is controlled but no longer sets anyone else's pace, and no one salutes her departure.
