## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Give the audience a complete danger map before the last negotiation begins. **Diễn xuất:** Mây's calm is intentional, Sơn's restraint is visible in his refusal to step past her.
- **2:** Expose Kiên's shrinking options while keeping the hostage and weapon physically distinct. **Diễn xuất:** The smooth voice has become clipped, his practical demand revealing fear without a sudden comic collapse.
- **3:** Make negotiation buy the exact attention Hạ needs, not merely pause action for dialogue. **Diễn xuất:** Her line offers a first achievable action, no taunt about his weakness or certainty he will not shoot.
- **4:** Let recognition of Sơn's changed hands become a shared practical understanding, not another romantic speech. **Diễn xuất:** Her fear turns into concentration; his response is a barely perceptible readiness, not an exaggerated nod.
- **5:** Show that the light will be a coordinated action using established equipment. **Diễn xuất:** His radio report is controlled and quiet, not anticipation acted as a grin or accidental switch.
- **6:** Move Hạ into a safer geometry through a plausible request before the distraction comes. **Diễn xuất:** Her dizziness is useful restraint rather than a fainting performance; Kiên adjusts because he still needs her upright.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Keep the established gallery wind and faint cable rattle. Backup light turns on only on the cue at 51.1.
