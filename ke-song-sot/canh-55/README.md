## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Make Sơn initiate an account that can cost him, even after the rescue earned sympathy. **Diễn xuất:** He meets her eyes without the guarded half-smile of the opening, she remains professional and attentive.
- **2:** Replace the nickname's mystique with a specific address and a remembered human consequence. **Diễn xuất:** He speaks plainly with effortful precision, avoiding a stylized killer's recital or dramatic stare into camera.
- **3:** Keep responsibility from becoming another elegant line that excuses the speaker. **Diễn xuất:** The admission is uncomfortable and direct, with no self-pity smile and no demand that Mây forgive him.
- **4:** Make the account lead outward to verification rather than stay inside Sơn's personal mythology. **Diễn xuất:** She does not soften the question because he helped Hạ, Sơn listens without defending his reputation.
- **5:** Show commitment becoming a signed record rather than another promise to do better. **Diễn xuất:** His concentration is ordinary and sober; the small fatigue in the LEFT wrist remains without obstructing the signature.
- **6:** Let rest follow accountability instead of treating unconsciousness as redemption. **Diễn xuất:** Exhaustion replaces vigilance gradually; no smile implies that his consequences have vanished.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Carry the station fan from the quiet holding room into morning evidence handover. Do not replay the statement or signature.
