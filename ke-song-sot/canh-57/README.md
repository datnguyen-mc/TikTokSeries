## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Let Hạ claim the right to invite someone into her own space again. **Diễn xuất:** The line has a small reclaiming humour through fatigue, not a declaration that all trauma is gone.
- **2:** Make inhabiting the house a concrete first task, not another memorial scene. **Diễn xuất:** Her attention is practical, a brief look at the photograph can inform the work without becoming a grief performance.
- **3:** Translate Hạ's earlier wish into a choice of dimensions and a workable place. **Diễn xuất:** Hạ is engaged in making something viable, Mây listens as a friend instead of an approving official.
- **4:** Make Hạ's independence a working decision rather than an inspirational slogan delivered to camera. **Diễn xuất:** The answer is plain and certain, with a trace of humour toward her friend, not defiance aimed at Sơn.
- **5:** Choose one personal farewell object without restarting the whole flower-shop romance. **Diễn xuất:** Hands move with a florist's everyday familiarity, not as if preparing a funeral offering.
- **6:** Let Hạ choose a safe visible place for the farewell instead of repeating a desperate chase. **Diễn xuất:** Her line is confident but restrained, choosing to be seen rather than demanding the vehicle stop.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Keep the approaching transport engine into Sơn window-side view. Hạ already holds the single lily; do not wrap it again.
