## Chỉ đạo diễn xuất từng clip

- **1:** Keep the cabin part of a working town rather than a monument reserved for Sơn. **Diễn xuất:** Bảy's practical line refuses a shrine without dismissing what happened, Mây accepts the ordinary future use.
- **2:** Preserve the fact of seven days without starting a new ritual of waiting. **Diễn xuất:** Her hands are professional and gentle, not hovering over a potential eighth cut or secret message.
- **3:** Bring Hạ into the cabin through work she has chosen, not through waiting for its former occupant. **Diễn xuất:** Her energy is practical and a little hopeful, Bảy responds by clearing useful space rather than giving a blessing speech.
- **4:** Make support between ordinary people tangible through a table that must actually stand. **Diễn xuất:** Bảy's line is dry practical advice, Hạ responds through adjusting her stance rather than another spoken joke.
- **5:** Move the marks into a useful future object while keeping their past meaning modest. **Diễn xuất:** Hạ's line is matter-of-fact remembrance, not a promise to count years until Sơn returns.
- **6:** Use collective work as a time bridge, not one more farewell tableau. **Diễn xuất:** Concentration and small cooperative adjustments replace dialogue, no one stops to look toward the road for Sơn.

**Âm nối sang cảnh sau:** Match the newly sanded table surface to the finished flower counter years later, with a clear morning/time change and older faces.
