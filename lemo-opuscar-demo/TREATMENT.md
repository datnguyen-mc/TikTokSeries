# Lemo-Opuscar demo — "Một câu lệnh" (One sentence)

Style: Stick-figure Explainer (`stick-explainer`). 1920×1080, 60 fps (STYLE.md §5), ~29 s.
Language: Vietnamese on screen only. No narration, no subtitles (titles carry the script). Music + foley.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The ripple.** One figure (you) types one sentence; a pulse leaves them along the ground line and wakes up a studio of figures, who join hands into a pipeline; the camera pulls back to 99 rows; everyone turns to face you.
2. **Before / after.** A figure alone tries to do everything (draw, score, cut) and drops it all ("let go"); then the plugin: the same jobs are spread along a chain that holds.
3. **Countdown.** Five figures, each a step; one sits down as its step is done until only the finished film is left standing.

**Chosen: 1, the ripple.** It is the shortest way to say what the plugin does (one sentence → an agent that directs, draws, scores and mixes), it ends on the user (the plugin is about *your* story), and its opening (a single figure on an empty line) and ending (everyone turns to one person) differ from the library demo's chain-between-two-ends and globe.

## 2. Logline and arc

You write one sentence, and a whole little studio springs up to make your film.
- Setup: one figure, one line, one sentence.
- Turn: the sentence becomes a pulse; four crew figures pop up and clasp hands into a pipeline; the accent runs through them.
- Ending: pull back to 99 small figures (the 99 styles); silence; all of them turn to face the one accent figure — you. Title card.

## 3. Benchmark

- Short motion-graphic product explainers built on one accent colour and type on a margin (editorial explainer channels, Swiss motion type).
- Learn: one idea per screen, the line of figures as the diagram, holds long enough to read, a silence before the payoff.
- Don't take: characters, layouts, melodies, logos, typefaces.

## 5. Beat sheet (100 bpm, beat 0.6 s, bar 2.4 s)

- 0.0 paper, empty ground line; 0.3 figure pops up (blip).
- 0.9 small label "BẠN" (you) under it; 1.2 a typed prompt line appears: "Làm một phim ngắn về quán cà phê của mẹ." (typewriter ticks, full text reported to readcheck from the start).
- 3.0 big title left: "Một câu lệnh." 
- 4.8 head turns accent; pulse leaves along the ground line (filtered noise sweep L→R).
- 5.4 / 6.0 / 6.6 / 7.2 crew pop up as the pulse passes: ĐẠO DIỄN, HỌA SĨ, NHẠC SĨ, DỰNG PHIM (rising blips + glockenspiel).
- 7.2 title: "AI của bạn thành một xưởng phim nhỏ."
- 9.6–12.0 hands clasp one by one, each on a beat (kalimba rising), soft palm taps.
- 12.0 accent run from you to the last crew member; the four steps light up.
- 12.0 title: "Đạo diễn, vẽ, soạn nhạc, hòa âm — tất cả bằng code."
- 14.4 pull back: the chain becomes one of many rows; big number counts 1 → 99 "phong cách phim" (marimba tally, harp sweep).
- 19.2 near-silence (room tone only), crowd breathes.
- 21.6 downbeat: all 99 turn to face the accent figure (one vibraphone chord + soft strings).
- 22.2 title card: "Lemo-Opuscar" / "Plugin làm phim cho Claude — chỉ cần một câu."
- 24.0 small install line: `claude plugin install lemo-opuscar@lemolab`.
- 28.8 end.
