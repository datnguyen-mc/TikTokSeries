# Cảnh 01: Hốc Lò Bầu Bịt Gạch Cuối Bãi Tha Ma & Bức Tượng Đất Không Mắt (6 Clips × 10s = 60s)

- **Thời gian:** 17:45 Chiều tà âm u (00:00–01:00)
- **Bối cảnh:** Abandoned ancient brick pottery kiln house at the edge of a desolate riverbank graveyard (xưởng lò bầu gốm cổ bỏ hoang cuối bãi tha ma ven sông vắng), soot-blackened brick dome sealed with red threads.
- **Trục không gian cố định:** Axis from the leaf-strewn rotting brick courtyard straight into the soot-blackened brick kiln dome whose arched mouth is bricked shut and crisscrossed with rotting blood-red threads under blood-red twilight.
- **Đạo cụ chính:** Bricked-up ancient pottery kiln dome with rotting red threads, handheld 3D LiDAR scanner emitting a green laser grid, faceless unglazed terracotta clay infant effigy with deep cinnabar spine slashes, coarse brown cloth.
