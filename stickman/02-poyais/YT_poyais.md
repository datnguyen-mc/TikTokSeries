**Description**
```
In 1822, Gregor MacGregor arrived in London calling himself the "Cazique" — a prince — of Poyais, a rich nation in Central America. There was just one problem: Poyais didn't exist.

A 355-page guidebook described a capital city called St. Joseph and rivers with "globules of pure gold." Investors bought land for a few shillings an acre, and MacGregor raised a £200,000 loan in London.

Roughly 250 settlers sailed for their new home… and found only jungle. At least 180 of them died. MacGregor tried the same scheme again in France — and was acquitted.
```
