## Description
In a thousand years, your descendants might not live on Earth… or even have a body.

A thousand years is only about 40 generations, which is far too short for evolution to change us much. The big changes will come from technology instead: gene editing (the first gene-editing therapy was approved in 2023 for sickle cell disease), lab-grown organs, brain chips, Mars colonies with 38% of Earth's gravity, spinning space cities, and minds uploaded into computers.

Could humanity split into Earth humans, Martians, and something else entirely?
