## Description
Six years. More than thirty nations. Over 100 million people in uniform. The biggest war in human history in 12 stories: the seeds of war after Versailles, Blitzkrieg, the miracle of Dunkirk, the Battle of Britain, the Russian winter, Pearl Harbor, ten minutes at Midway, Stalingrad, cracking Enigma, D-Day and the Ghost Army, the fall of Berlin, and the day the world fell silent.
