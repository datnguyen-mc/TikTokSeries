## Description
“Home before the leaves fall” – yet the war lasted four years and killed more than 16 million people. Twelve true stories: the taxis of the Marne, life in the trenches, the 1914 Christmas Truce, poison gas at Ypres, the Lusitania, Verdun, the first tanks at the Somme, the Zimmermann Telegram, Cher Ami the hero pigeon, the 1918 flu, and the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month.

## Notes
- Real people are shown as neutral stick figures, not likenesses; casualties are depicted with restraint.
- Figures are rounded from the sources below; casualty numbers are estimates. “Home before the leaves fall” is attributed to the Kaiser.
- The Marne taxis carried around 5,000 men – their symbolic impact was far greater than their military one.
