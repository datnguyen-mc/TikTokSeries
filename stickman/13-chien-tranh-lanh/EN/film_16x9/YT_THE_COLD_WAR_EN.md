## Description
For 45 years, the two superpowers never fired directly at each other – yet the whole world lived on the edge of nuclear war. Twelve true stories: the Iron Curtain, the Berlin candy bombers, duck and cover, Sputnik's beep, the U-2 shot down, Gagarin's 108 minutes, the wall that appeared overnight, the 13 days of the Cuban Missile Crisis, one small step on the Moon, ping-pong diplomacy, the Miracle on Ice, and the day the red flag came down.
