## Description
Most of us work until 65. But in America and other developed countries, some people retire at 30 or 40. Follow Alex – a fictional software developer – through 12 chapters: a book from 1992, the 4% rule, the savings rate, the big three expenses, index funds, the 2020 crash, Lean/Fat/Barista/Coast FIRE, the rules in the UK, Germany, Japan and Australia, the last day at the office – and the dark side of freedom.

⚠️ Alex is a fictional character; the numbers and the story of the FIRE movement are real. This video is for storytelling and education only and is NOT financial or investment advice.
