## Description
At 1:23 AM on April 26, 1986, reactor number four at the Chernobyl nuclear power plant exploded. In 12 chapters: the city of Pripyat, the fateful test, the firefighters, the evacuation, the secret uncovered in Sweden, the helicopter pilots, the three divers, 600,000 liquidators, the trial, the exclusion zone and the giant steel arch 40 years later.

⚠️ Stick figures and AI illustrations are used to retell historical events; real people appear only as stick figures with name tags. Some figures (such as the death toll) are still disputed between sources.

## Notes
- Death toll estimates vary widely: 31 (initial official figure), ~4,000 projected long-term cancer deaths (WHO / Chernobyl Forum 2005), and much higher figures in some other studies.
- The story that the three divers died is a popular myth – all three survived the mission.
- The account of people watching the fire from a bridge comes from eyewitness stories and is not fully verified.
- All images are animated illustrations, not archive photos.
