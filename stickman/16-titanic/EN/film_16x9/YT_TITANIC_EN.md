## Description
At 11:40 PM on April 14, 1912, the Titanic – the largest ship in the world – struck an iceberg. In 12 chapters: the 'unsinkable' ship, a floating city divided into three classes, the ignored warnings, 37 fateful seconds, 'women and children first', the SOS, the band that played to the end, the Carpathia's race through the ice, the SOLAS convention and the wreck on the ocean floor 114 years later.

⚠️ Stick figures and AI illustrations are used to retell historical events; real people appear only as stick figures with name tags. This film remembers the more than 1,500 people who died and does not depict casualties.

## Notes
- Survivor and death counts differ between sources (about 705–712 survivors; about 1,490–1,517 dead).
- Survival rates by class (≈97% of first-class women, ≈16% of third-class men) are based on Encyclopedia Titanica lists; other sources differ by a few percent.
- How far the Californian really was from Titanic that night is still debated.
- All images are animated illustrations, not archive photos.
