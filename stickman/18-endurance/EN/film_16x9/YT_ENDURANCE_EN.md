## Description
In 1915, Ernest Shackleton's wooden ship Endurance was crushed by the ice of the Weddell Sea. Twenty-eight men were stranded on drifting ice, more than 500 km from land, and no one in the world knew where they were. In 12 chapters: the dream of crossing Antarctica, a winter locked in the ice, the day the ship sank, months on a drifting floe, Elephant Island, 17 days in the James Caird, the crossing of South Georgia, four rescue attempts – and the wreck found 3,008 metres down in 2022.

⚠️ This video uses stick figures and AI-generated background art to retell historical events; real people appear only as labelled stick figures. No bodies or injuries are shown.

## Notes (add to description)
- The famous "Men wanted for hazardous journey" advert has never been found and is most likely a legend.
- Sources differ on how many of Hurley's glass plates were kept (~120–150).
- The wreck lay about 4 nautical miles (~7 km) from Worsley's recorded position, at 3,008 m (announced 9 March 2022).
- Three men of the Ross Sea party died; all 28 men of the Endurance survived.
