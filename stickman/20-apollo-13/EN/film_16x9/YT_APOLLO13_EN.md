## Description
In April 1970, more than 300,000 km from Earth, an oxygen tank exploded aboard Apollo 13. The power faded, oxygen vented into space, and three astronauts had to shelter in a lunar module built for two.

How do you fit a square filter into a round hole with plastic bags, cardboard and duct tape? How do you steer by hand when the computer is switched off? And why did a few minutes of silence on the way home make the whole world hold its breath?

## Notes on content
- Explosion: evening of 13 April 1970 (Houston time), about 330,000 km from Earth; oxygen tank 2 in the service module.
- The 400,171 km record (15 April 1970) was surpassed by Artemis II on 6 April 2026.
- Cause per the Review Board: tank dropped in 1968, thermostatic switches rated 28 V on a 65 V ground supply, heater left on ~8 hours during the March 1970 detanking.
- Grumman's "towing invoice" was a joke, not a real bill.
