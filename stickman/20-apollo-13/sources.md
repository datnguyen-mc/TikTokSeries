## Ghi chú số liệu / notes on figures
- Explosion: 13 April 1970, ~55 h 54 min into the flight, ~330,000 km from Earth; O2 tank 2 in the service module.
- Fan stir command ~95 s before the bang (GET 55:53:18 → 55:54:53).
- Cause (Review Board): tank 2 dropped ~5 cm in 1968; thermostatic switches rated 28 V not upgraded for the 65 V ground supply; heater left on ~8 h during the March 1970 detanking, damaging wire insulation.
- Record: 400,171 km (248,655 mi) from Earth on 15 April 1970; surpassed by Artemis II on 6 April 2026 (56 years).
- LM "lifeboat": designed for 2 people for ~2 days; carried 3 for ~4 days. Water ~180 ml (6 oz) per person per day was the commonly cited ration; cabin temperatures fell to ~3 °C.
- PC+2 burn ~4 min 24 s; mid-course correction by hand ~14 s using Earth's terminator as reference.
- Blackout during reentry lasted noticeably longer than expected (about 1.5 min longer); Swigert's first words after blackout: "Okay, Joe."
- Splashdown 17 April 1970, South Pacific, ~6.5 km from USS Iwo Jima.
