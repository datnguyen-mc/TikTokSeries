## Notes
- This episode is a thought experiment: the Moon simply vanishes, with no explosion and no debris. Long-term effects (tilt, day length) are model estimates and scientists still debate their size.
- The 1993 study (Laskar et al.) suggested a moonless Earth's tilt could swing between about 0° and 85°; later simulations (e.g. Lissauer et al. 2012) found slower and smaller swings.
- Estimates for the length of today's day if the Moon had never formed vary widely (roughly 6–12 hours in some studies); the figure is shown as uncertain.
