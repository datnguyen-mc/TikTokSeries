## Notes
- What happened to the ten people aboard is unknown. The fume and faulty-chronometer/pump explanations are modern hypotheses, not proven facts.
- The dinner between Briggs and Morehouse is reported in several accounts but is presented here as "according to one story".
- The half-eaten breakfast and warm tea come from fiction (Arthur Conan Doyle's 1884 story) and later retellings, not from the 1872–73 testimony.
- Real people (the Briggs family, crew, Morehouse, Deveau, Wright, Solly-Flood, Conan Doyle, Andrea Sella) appear only as simple labelled drawings.
