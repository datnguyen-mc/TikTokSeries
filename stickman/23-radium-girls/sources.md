## Notes
- The New Jersey case was settled out of court in June 1928 ($10,000 each, a yearly payment and medical costs). Catherine Donohue won her Illinois case in April 1938; appeals by Radium Dial ended in 1939.
- The Harvard (Drinker) report concluded radium was the likely cause; a version given to New Jersey officials claimed the workers were in perfect condition, which Alice Hamilton helped expose.
- Real people (the Curies, Mollie Maggia, Grace Fryer, Raymond Berry, Cecil Drinker, Alice Hamilton, Catherine Donohue) appear only as simple labelled drawings. Medical details are kept non-graphic.
