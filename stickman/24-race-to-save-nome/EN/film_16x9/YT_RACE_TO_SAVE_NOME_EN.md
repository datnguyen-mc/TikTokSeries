## Description
January 1925. In Nome, Alaska, children are falling ill with diphtheria – and the only medicine that can save them is 674 miles away, across frozen wilderness, with no roads and the sea frozen solid.

This is the true story of the 1925 serum run: twenty mushers and about 150 sled dogs, a relay through brutal cold, a 12-year-old lead dog named Togo crossing a frozen sea, a blizzard that flipped the sled – and Balto, the dog who became a legend.

## Notes on content
- Mileage for Seppala & Togo is given as 261–264 miles depending on the source; Balto's team ran the final ~53 miles.
- Dates follow the main accounts: the relay left Nenana on January 27 and reached Nome about 5:30 am on February 2, 1925.
