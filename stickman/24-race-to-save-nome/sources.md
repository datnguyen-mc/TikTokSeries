## Notes
- Distances: 674 miles Nenana → Nome; 20 mushers, ~150 dogs; relay Jan 27 – Feb 2, 1925 (about 5½ days).
- Seppala & Togo's total is given as 261–264 miles depending on the source; Kaasen & Balto ran the final ~53 miles (some sources say 55).
- Balto's statue in Central Park was unveiled in December 1925 with Balto present.
