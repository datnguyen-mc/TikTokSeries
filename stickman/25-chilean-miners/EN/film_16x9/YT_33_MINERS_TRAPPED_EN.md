## Description
August 5, 2010. Deep under the Atacama Desert, a mountain of rock collapses inside the San José mine. Thirty-three men are trapped 700 metres down, with food for only a couple of days – and nobody knows if they are alive.

This is how they survived: two spoonfuls of tuna every two days, a note in red ink tied to a drill, three giant drills racing at once, and a capsule just 54 centimetres wide.

👉 Could you have survived 17 days on two spoonfuls of tuna? Tell me in the comments!
