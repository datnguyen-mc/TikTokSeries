## Notes
- Collapse: August 5, 2010; 33 men trapped ~700 m down, ~5 km from the entrance; refuge ~50 m², ~31 °C.
- Contact on August 22 (day 17) through a borehole at 688 m; note "Estamos bien en el refugio los 33".
- Plan B (Schramm T130XD) broke through October 9 at 08:05; capsule Fénix 2 (~54 cm wide) built by the Chilean Navy shipyard ASMAR with NASA input.
- Rescue: first descent late on October 12; first miner up just after midnight on October 13; last miner up at 21:55–21:56 on October 13.
- Miners are not named on screen (private individuals); they appear as unnamed figures.
