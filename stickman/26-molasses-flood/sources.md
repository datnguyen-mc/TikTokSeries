## Notes
- January 15, 1919, about 12:30 pm; tank built 1915 on Commercial Street; ~2.3 million gallons; wave up to ~25 ft at ~35 mph; 21 dead, ~150 injured.
- Leaks were reportedly painted over in brown; the tank was tested with only a few inches of water.
- The company (United States Industrial Alcohol) claimed anarchist sabotage; the auditor's 1925 ruling found the tank unsafe; settlements of about $628,000.
- 2016 Harvard study (corn-syrup model in a cold room) explained the deadly thickening.
