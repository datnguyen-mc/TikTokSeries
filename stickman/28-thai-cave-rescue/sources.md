## Notes
- June 23 – July 10, 2018 (18 days); 12 boys aged 11–16 and a 25-year-old assistant coach; found July 2 about 4 km in.
- Former Thai Navy SEAL Saman Kunan died on July 6, 2018 while placing air tanks.
- Rescue days July 8, 9 and 10; four stateless members were granted Thai citizenship in September 2018.
- The boys and their coach are not named on screen (private individuals).
