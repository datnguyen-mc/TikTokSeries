## Description
August 1590. John White finally returns to the English settlement on Roanoke Island after three years away – and finds it empty. More than a hundred men, women and children, including his daughter and baby granddaughter Virginia Dare, have vanished.

The only clue: a single word carved into a wooden post. CROATOAN.

In this episode: the secret signal, the Spanish Armada that kept White away, the storm that stopped him reaching Croatoan, and the leading theories – from Hatteras Island to a hidden fort on White's own map.

## Notes on content
- About 115 colonists arrived in 1587; Virginia Dare was born August 18, 1587.
- White returned on August 18, 1590 and found CROATOAN carved on a post and CRO on a tree, with no cross.
- Site X follows the 2012 British Museum study of White's 'La Virginea Pars' map; the pottery could also have other explanations.
- The Dare Stones (from 1937 onward) are widely considered fakes.
