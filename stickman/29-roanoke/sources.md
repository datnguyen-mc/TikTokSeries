## Notes
- 1585 first colony; 1587 second colony of ~115 under John White; Virginia Dare born August 18, 1587.
- White returned August 18, 1590; CROATOAN on a palisade post, CRO on a tree; no cross; a storm prevented the trip to Croatoan (Hatteras).
- Site X: 2012 British Museum study of 'La Virginea Pars'; drought 1587–89 from tree rings; Dare Stones considered fakes.
