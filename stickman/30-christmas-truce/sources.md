## Notes
- 24–26 December 1914 (in places until the New Year); about 100,000 British and German troops.
- Candles on Christmas trees and "Stille Nacht" on Christmas Eve; gifts, photos and joint burials on Christmas Day; football kickabouts in some places.
- Pope Benedict XV called for a truce on 7 December 1914; it was not officially adopted.
- 2014 centenary: British Army vs German Army football match.
