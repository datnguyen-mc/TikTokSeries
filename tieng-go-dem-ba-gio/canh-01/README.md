- **Thời gian:** 18:30 Chập tối tháng Bảy âm lịch (00:00–01:00)
- **Bối cảnh:** Mossy laterite stone archway gate, rain-swept Bát Tràng brick courtyard with the chained laterite well, and dark ironwood veranda of the 150-year-old Vũ clan ancestral house.
- **Trục không gian cố định:** Axis from the mossy stone gate across the wet brick courtyard past the chained laterite well to the dimly lit three-bay ironwood veranda.
- **Đạo cụ chính:** Mossy stone archway gate with iron bars, laterite stone well sealed with a red-inscribed stone slab and three rusted iron chains, black oiled-paper umbrella, bronze key ring, vintage contract paper and red ink pad.
